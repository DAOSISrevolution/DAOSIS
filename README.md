# 🌍 DAOSIS: Decentralized Automatically Optimizing Self‑Improvement System

**DAOSIS** is a decentralized, open-source ecosystem that turns real-world outcomes into shared intelligence. It learns from *evidence* — not ads — to route people to the best **methods, people, places, and tools** and to coordinate real transformation with lower cost and higher success.

## 🚀 What is DAOSIS?
Start with an **AI-guided goal interview**: the system makes your goal SMART, sets baselines, and builds a plan with **progress checks, weekly practices, daily habits, and motivation anchors** — then keeps you accountable with reminders and adaptive suggestions.

Then DAOSIS layers in:
- 📊 **Awareness** — Scores trainers, methods, spaces, and tools (supplements/devices) by real outcomes (effectiveness, cost, quality, safety).
- 🤝 **Connection** — Match with the right professionals, peers, products, and **spaces** (gyms/studios and bookable homes).
- 🧠 **Coordination** — AI finds the **best times**, books rooms/classes, splits costs, and automates **payments/scheduling**.
- 🎯 **Focus** — **No ads, no selling, no irrelevant feeds** — only what advances your chosen goal.
- 🪙 **Reward** — Earn **Transformation Coin** for impact; revenue-backed and tied to DAO voting.

**Result:** more transformation in less time for less money — while **entrepreneurs & space owners earn more** by cutting middlemen and marketing overhead. DAOSIS is a living research platform that **shifts power to evidence and community**.
